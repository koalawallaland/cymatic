About the studios

The slime bar, vats, lake, falls, mirrors and communal slime wall take inspiration from Sloomoo Los Angeles. The squishy casting bench and wax workshop are original Sunny Place inventions. Making happens on screen. Real wax heating and silicone casting are grown-up jobs.
