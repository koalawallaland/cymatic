# Squish station

Static browser adaptation of Katie's The Sunny Place originals, supplied as SquishWaxAssets.zip and SquishWaxExport.rbxmx. The included glTF has 21 usable SquishyItems and the original WaxBar. No unavailable models or private audio were substituted.

Open `index.html` through an HTTP server. Three.js 0.170.0 is vendored with its MIT license. The original glTF is gzip-compressed without changing its contents; the browser decompresses it before loading embedded meshes and textures. The exported plastic materials are rendered with metalness 0, and the toys face the viewer.

The original SquishScript's 0.62 squeeze depth, 0.55 lateral expansion, 90 ms press and 1.6 second recovery are adapted for the browser. WaxServer supplies the six wax colors, rainbow palette, 110-piece progression, first-crack sound and three crackles. Multipart toys receive a rounded translucent coat. The unavailable splat sound is replaced by a soft synthesized splat, as authorized. Selection is saved locally; no uploads or accounts are involved.

Manual verification: all 21 selections; search; squeeze button and keyboard; dip; first crack; repeated removal and full reveal; reset; color choice; sound toggle; saved selection; original wax-bar preview; desktop and 390px layout. Existing chess and graffiti files were not changed.
