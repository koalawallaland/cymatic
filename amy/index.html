POAMS: the quantitative cross-check

The 2021 NASA TM’s V3 test (7 October 2016) used 211 g of bismuth, with apparent scale changes about ±0.3 g and 0.01 g scale resolution. The magnet field was reported at 2,000 gauss (0.2 T). The signal faded and returned after magnet replacement; thermal and aerodynamic artifacts remained plausible. Direct spin-polarization data were not reported. V5 equipment/data were held by Quantum Machines LLC at the time of the report. Scale resolution alone is not an uncertainty budget.

X = (mvo²/ro)(spωp/ro)
Δf ≈ ±X/(2FN) − X²/(8FN³) + …

INDEPENDENT CALCULATION This expansion of Eq. 28a assumes |X| ≪ FN² and a positive Newtonian force magnitude. Equal ± signs cancel the leading odd term, not the even term. The report’s classical spin representation also encounters potentially superluminal nuclear surface speeds, which it acknowledges. Neither issue establishes the proposed force.

Strong counter-evidence

  • 2016 opposite-spin rubidium test: ηS=(0.2±1.2)×10⁻⁷, model-dependent torsion-gradient bound 5.4×10⁻⁶ m⁻².
  • 2024 atom-interferometry constraints: about 10⁻¹⁰ on specified anomalous gravitational mass tensor terms.
  • 2023 neutron spin energy limit: 5.3×10⁻²² eV (95% confidence) for the interaction tested.
  • Molecular fifth-force spectroscopy: g²≲1.9×10⁻¹⁵ at λ=10 nm for the specified Yukawa model. V₅=−N₁N₂g²ℏc exp(−r/λ)/(4πr).
  • 1999 NASA YBCO gravity test: changes below 2×10⁻⁸ g; 2024 steady electromagnetic fields and Alzofon NMR study: null results and thermal artifacts under tested conditions.

Hypothesis register — not Amy’s equation

H = H₀ + HEM + HSO + Hhf − λs Ŝ·ĝ

TOY MODEL Here Ŝ and ĝ are unit directions for spin and gravity, and λs has energy units. This parameterization isolates a putative spin–gravity term from ordinary electromagnetic, spin–orbit and hyperfine terms. It is not a derivation or claim of a new force. Spectroscopic signals and independent mechanical measurements would need to exclude magnetic gradients, heating, convection, scale drift and other known effects in matched blinded experiments. The previously suggested bismuth T₁ enhancement, Nieh–Yan/6D projection, time-crystal phase memory and phase-transition bridge remain unverified hypotheses.
