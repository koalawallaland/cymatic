Quick walkthrough

  1. Select an empty PCB socket.
  2. Choose a switch type and press Install.
  3. Solder its joint, then press Test. Repeat across the board.
  4. Swap a switch by desoldering it first.

Ready. Select a socket to begin.

Progress is saved in this browser only. Use a real temperature-controlled iron, ventilation, protective equipment, and the manufacturer's instructions for any physical assembly. The simulation does not verify electrical safety.
