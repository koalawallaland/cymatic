Paths to Inner Power · an annotated collection

Unearthly
Archives

An intimate library of consciousness, symbolism, material culture and the history of occult inquiry. Open any volume to explore its ideas and the questions it leaves behind.

27 titles from the Aquarian Press advertisement, plus two foundational psychometry texts. The summaries are original reading notes based on catalog records, available descriptions and historical context; they are not full-text chapter summaries. Occult theories are presented as historical claims, not established findings. Cover designs are original, inspired by the era rather than reproductions.

UNEARTHLY
ARCHIVESThe Soul of Things✳No. 28 · KT
The Soul of ThingsWilliam & Elizabeth Denton · 1863Open volume ↘

Historical primary-source overview

Inside the book

The Dentons assembled accounts of psychometric experiments in which objects or geological specimens were treated as potential sources of impressions about distant events. The volume discusses Buchanan, historical reconstruction and the idea that matter records its surroundings. It is a primary historical account, not independent confirmation of its claims.

Research prompt

Read the experimental descriptions and ask what information was available to the reader, how successes and misses were counted, and what could falsify the hypothesis.

Manual of PsychometryJoseph Rodes BuchananOpen volume ↘

Historical primary-source overview

Inside the book

Subtitled The Dawn of a New Civilization, this nineteenth-century work presents Buchanan's system of psychometry, which he conceived as a way to measure and investigate the soul through perceived impressions. It traces a foundational belief that subjective sensitivity could serve as an instrument of inquiry.

Research prompt

Compare its claims with the principles of blinding, measurement and reproducibility used in present-day experimental design.
