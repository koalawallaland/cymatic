The exact same audio buffer played both times, at the same speed. It contains generated noise shaped with speech-like resonances and a syllabic rhythm. There is no spoken sentence or hidden message.

If the suggestion changed what you heard, that is an observation about interpretation. If it did not, that is an equally valid result. This small, unblinded demonstration cannot measure how suggestible you are or establish a paranormal cause.

Inside the archive

EVP means “electronic voice phenomenon”: a label used for apparent voices in recordings. This room borrows that atmosphere for an exploratory demonstration of auditory pattern finding and suggestion. It is not evidence of spirits.

The clip is procedural fiction rather than an authentic field recording. A seeded generator produces the same voice-like noise for every playback in a session. The phrase is chosen independently. You may hear a phrase, another phrase, or no speech at all; no particular effect is promised.

No infrasound experiment, binaural beats, sudden scares, microphone access or uploaded responses. Your notes stay in this page’s memory and clear when you leave or reset.
