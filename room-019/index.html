The case file: what the research actually says

Tandy and Lawrence’s 1998 paper proposed that a roughly 19 Hz standing wave might explain Tandy’s peripheral apparition. The frequency was estimated from room dimensions; the authors explicitly lacked a sound-pressure measurement. The eye-resonance mechanism was a hypothesis, not a demonstrated causal result.

A later controlled “Haunt” study of 79 participants found reported anomalous experiences unrelated to the experimental infrasound/EMF condition. That does not rule out every possible acoustic effect, but it does not support a reliable ghost-producing frequency.

Moving an eye can change an image; it does not establish a new ability to detect otherwise invisible beings.
