Middle Pillar Invocation

Invocation steps

Sit with your feet on the floor and let your breath settle. Picture a thread of light descending through five points. Pause for an easy breath at each. You can intone each name softly or say it inwardly.

  1. Kether · Above the crown

    Picture a small sphere of white light just above your head.

    Eheieh
    eh-hey-yay

  2. Daath · Nape of the neck

    Let a thread of light descend from the crown and form a sphere at the nape.

    YHVH Elohim
    yode-heh-vav-heh el-oh-heem

  3. Tiphareth · Center of the chest

    Follow the thread down to the center of your chest. Imagine the sphere glowing like sunlight.

    YHVH Eloah ve-Daath
    yode-heh-vav-heh el-oh-ah v’dah-ath

  4. Yesod · Pelvis

    Let the light descend to the pelvis and gather into a sphere there.

    Shaddai El Chai
    shah-dye el-khai

  5. Malkuth · Feet

    Bring the thread down to your feet and picture the last sphere where they meet the floor.

    Adonai ha-Aretz
    ah-doe-nye ha-ah-retz

Close the practice

Picture all five lights connected for a breath or two. Let the image fade and feel your feet on the floor.

For a quiet scry, wait a few seconds. If an image, word, or sensation comes up, jot it down as you experienced it.

Adapted from Israel Regardie’s Middle Pillar exercise.
