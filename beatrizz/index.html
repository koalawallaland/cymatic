Entry 001 · 18 Nov 2026

Verified research

The Palomar plate debate

Villarroel and coauthors dispute the sample selection, timing information and statistical power in a reassessment of historical Palomar plate features. Watters and coauthors argue that dataset problems and the telescope’s observing schedule undermine the claimed Earth-shadow and nuclear-test associations. The two manuscripts disagree about the analysis; the origin of the candidate features remains unresolved.

The research sources are checkable. Their competing interpretations are not settled facts, and this exchange does not establish artificial objects.

Entry 002 · 26 Nov 2026

Inside a 1996 Japanese PlayStation UFO archive

Reddit contributor u/mattperkins86 describes extracting stills from eight sections of a Japanese PlayStation disc. The examples range from scans of documents and sketches to photographs of crop circles and images of the Cydonia region of Mars. Some are photographs of other photographs. For anyone tracing an image, the game offers a place to look for an earlier copy.

The Japanese catalog lists 496 photos and 53 video items. Its figures describe the published collection. The counts above describe files in the uploader’s ZIP. The uploader also reports finding one sequence at four zoom levels, so four clips can depict the same event.

Where did the pictures come from before the game? The Reddit post traces the NASA story to Jesse Michels’ interview with Jōsen Takano. The Geometry of Time

A living research folio about spatial and temporal quasicrystals, silver-ratio scaling, Faraday waves, Lorentzian geometry and the Eskridge spin–gravity claims. How to read the evidence labels
Verified research
A scholarly source whose authors, date and text can be checked. The label does not certify its conclusions or imply peer review.
Reported
An attributed public statement or report, linked to its original source. An allegation remains an allegation.
Unverified discussion
An individual community post or comment, with its own URL and date. Independent corroboration is recorded separately.
