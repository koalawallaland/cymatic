## pages

Koala Walla Land starts at the desk. Every page is plain HTML with no build step.

- **`index.html`: the desk.** The landing page. Click things on it:
  - the monitor opens the studio
  - the resin bottles open the Resin Lab
  - the graffiti poster opens the wall
  - the hanging sign opens the shop
  - the sticky note opens the experiments
  - the keyboard, mug and handheld go to the kaps, café and play sections

  Click the neon kaomoji sign to change its face. The candle turns the lamp on and off.
- **`studio.html`: the studio.** The cymatic field instrument, as its own tab.
- **`lab/`: Mini Resin Lab.** Its neon signs are kaomoji too. `lab/part-c/` and `lab/classic/` are earlier versions.
- **`wall/`, `shop/`:** the graffiti wall, and the shop (closed for now).
- **`works/`:** every experiment: the keycap board, KeyKollectiv 65%, the ripple tank, and the first cymatic field.

The photos in `img/` are resized, and all their metadata (including any location data) has been removed.

## studio

Open `studio.html` in a browser. There is no build step and no dependencies.

- **♡ LIVE VOICE** asks for the microphone. Your pitch sets the frequency, your loudness sets the amplitude, and how clear your tone is sets the coherence.
- **modes**: Chladni (square-plate eigenmodes that blend from one (n, m) pair to the next), interference (point sources arranged in a ring), radial (circular-membrane modes), fluid (a warped standing wave).
- Sand particles get shaken off the antinodes and settle on the nodal lines, the way real Chladni sand does.
- **resonance** sharpens the nodal lines and speeds up settling. Lower **coherence** blurs the pattern with noise.
