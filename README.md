# Koala Walla Land / Cymatic Studio

**An interactive personal world, experimental sound studio, and evolving digital artwork.**

Enter through the animated shoji screens, explore a desk where objects become portals, experiment with sound and perception, and discover creative work embedded in the environment.

**[Enter the studio](https://koalawallaland.github.io/cymatic/)** · **[Visitor field guide](FIELD-GUIDE.md)** · **[Technical field notebook](TECHNICAL-NOTEBOOK.md)** · **[Quality checklist](QUALITY-CHECKLIST.md)**

## The concept

Instead of presenting creative work as a static gallery, the site turns a personal creative practice into an explorable place. The connective ideas are resonance, memory, attention, play, and the meeting point of digital and tactile experiences.

## Development

The project is personally art-directed and developed with AI-assisted coding tools. Creative direction, specifications, iteration, and testing are meaningful parts of its authorship; this is not a claim that every line was hand-coded or that every feature is complete.
