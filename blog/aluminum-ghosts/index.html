Aluminum.b Ghosts

page visits ···counting from Nov 8, 2026 · visits, not unique readers

I'll wrap the board in foil
and keep it in the freezer
at the edge of someone younger
is the place I tried to leave
her;

fidgeting hands
keep finding me
on a frequency
I can't turn off

ringing in my ears
humming from beneath
I can taste
her voice
like metal
in between
my teeth

hold one tone
and bend the rest
till something high
climbs out my chest

low one stays
high one leaves
the board still moves
when no one breathes

my throat
became
the planchette
to a board
I never owned

from a game
I only played
when I still
believed in ghosts

like a Freudian
slip, through time
she finds me

a most egregious host
