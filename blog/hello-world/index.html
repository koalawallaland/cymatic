Hello, world

This site is a home for writing and works in progress.

Posts are plain markdown files — this one lives at src/content/blog/hello-world.md. To publish a new post, add a new .md file to that folder with a title and date in the frontmatter, and it appears in the index and RSS feed automatically.

Interactive pieces get their own pages under /works, where there’s nothing between the code and the canvas.
