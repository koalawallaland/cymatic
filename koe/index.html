koe pocket STYLUS · SINGING VOICE

A pocket synth that sings. Touch the stylus to the key strip and she sings a syllable. Slide it along the strip and she glides between notes. The five vowel buttons change what she sings, VIBRATO makes her voice waver, and SHIFT switches the lyric mode. (koe is Japanese for "voice".)

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a longer key strip.

No note held

Tab to the keys, use ← / → to choose a note, and hold Space or Enter to play. Home / End go to the first / last key. Z / X shift the octave.

🎙 record the koala's voice

Sing

Press on the black strip and drag. On a computer keyboard, A–; is the bottom octave and W E T Y U O P are the sharps. Z/X move the octave.

Vowels

Tap あ い う え お (or 1–5), or press them while she's singing, to change the vowel. Watch her mouth change shape.

Modes

Hold SHIFT (Shift) and tap a vowel. い = VOWEL (sings the vowel you picked), う = DO·RE·MI (sings the note name), え = LYRIC (sings the lyric below the device, one syllable per note). The mode buttons under the device do the same thing.

🐨 Koala voice

Switch to koala voice and she sings with recorded syllables instead of the synthesized voice: any note, any lyric, with the koala on the faceplate. tone moves her voice from deeper to cuter. The voice is made in the recording booth.

🌿 Koala noises

Switch to koala noises and the keys stop singing. The low half of the strip is a bellow: a snoring breath in, then a belching breath out, for as long as you hold the key. The high half is the whine a female or a joey makes, which sounds like a sad kazoo sliding downhill. Slide between the two halves to morph one into the other, and hold VIBRATO to make it growl.

Expression

Hold VIBRATO (Space) for a deep vibrato. Long notes also pick up a soft natural vibrato on their own. ▲ ▼ set the volume. With SHIFT held, they change the octave.
