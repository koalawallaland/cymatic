Make your first song

You don't need to read music. A song is a short tune you repeat, a second tune that lifts, and words that fit. Give it about twenty minutes.

  1. Wake her upPress power on, open 🎹 Play, and pick Vocaloid voice. It can sing any syllable. (KT’s voice only knows a few syllables so far.)
  2. Find your five safe notesPlay only the keys marked C, D, E, G and A. These five sound good in any order, so you can't hit a wrong note. Start and finish on C. That's home.
  3. Make a hookTap four or five safe notes until a little pattern sticks in your head. Play it three times in a row. If you can hum it without looking, that's your chorus.
  4. Make a verseFind a second pattern that sits lower and moves less, more like talking. The verse tells the story. The chorus is the part people remember.
  5. Give it wordsWrite one short line for each pattern, with one syllable for every note. Press LYRIC and type the line in the lyric box as simple sounds, like ko a ra no u ta. Each key you press sings the next syllable.
  6. Put it in orderVerse, chorus, verse, chorus, then the chorus once more, ending on C. Type all the lines into the lyric box in that order. That is a whole song, about a minute long.
  7. Add feelingSlide between two keys instead of lifting. Hold the last note of each line and press VIBRATO. Play the last chorus higher with Octave +.
  8. Record itPress ● Record, play it start to finish, press ■ Stop, then ↓ Save audio. A wobbly first take still counts. Do three and keep the best one.

Stuck on words? Sing the whole thing on la la la first, get the tune right, then swap the words in.

What each control does

Sing

Press on the black strip and drag. On a computer keyboard, A–; is the bottom octave and W E T Y U O P are the sharps. Z/X move the octave.

Vowels

Tap あ い う え お (or 1–5), or press them while she's singing, to change the vowel. Watch her mouth change shape.

Modes

Hold SHIFT (Shift) and tap a vowel. い = VOWEL (sings the vowel you picked), う = DO·RE·MI (sings the note name), え = LYRIC (sings the lyric below the device, one syllable per note). The mode buttons under the device do the same thing.

🐨 Koala voice

Switch to koala voice and she sings with recorded syllables instead of the synthesized voice: any note, any lyric, with the koala on the faceplate. tone moves her voice from deeper to cuter. The voice is made in the recording booth.

🌿 Koala noises

Switch to koala noises and the keys stop singing. The low half of the strip is a bellow: a snoring breath in, then a belching breath out, for as long as you hold the key. The high half is the whine a female or a joey makes, which sounds like a sad kazoo sliding downhill. Slide between the two halves to morph one into the other, and hold VIBRATO to make it growl.

Expression

Hold VIBRATO (Space) for a deep vibrato. Long notes also pick up a soft natural vibrato on their own. ▲ ▼ set the volume. With SHIFT held, they change the octave.
