FURBY HUB

An original hatchery and control panel for your Furbies. Hatch Squishlings in the nursery, take them home to feed, play and potty-train, then connect the Furby Connect (Bluetooth) and Furby Boom (sound) so they cheer along. Works in Chrome. For the Furby Connect's Bluetooth on an iPad, open this page in the free Bluefy app.

Furby Boom Sound · experimental

The Boom has no Bluetooth. Its old app whispered commands as very high-pitched tones. Put the Boom's face near your Mac's speaker, keep volume low, press Wake him up, then try the buttons. The tone scheme was mapped for the 2012 Furby, so some Boom commands may not respond.
