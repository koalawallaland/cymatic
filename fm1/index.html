FM-1 6-OP FM · 32 ALGORITHMS

A working model of the pink M-VAVE FM-1 that lives under my desk. It has six sine operators, the 32 classic DX-style algorithms, 4-rate/4-level envelopes, a pitch envelope, an LFO, six effects you can put in any order, an arpeggiator and a 16-step sequencer. The controls work the way they do on the real one.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for bigger keys.

Play

Tap or drag across the keys. On a computer keyboard: Z–M and Q–O are the white keys, S D F H J and 2 3 4 6 7 9 the black ones. ←→ shift octaves. Press both OCT buttons to reset.

Knobs

Drag up or down, scroll, or focus one and use the arrow keys. PRESETS loads sounds 001–128, ALGORITHM swaps the operator wiring (watch the diagram), SELECT turns pages, and KNOB 1–4 edit whatever the screen shows. Hold OCT− or OCT+ and turn ALGORITHM to transpose.

Deep edit

Press EDIT and the labelled black keys light up. Choose OP1–OP6, PIT (pitch envelope), GLO (algorithm, feedback, sync, transpose, glide) or MONO / POLY. Each operator has six pages: rates, levels, tuning, sensitivity, and two pages of key scaling.

FX · ARP · SEQ

FX: SELECT moves between the six slots, knob 4 switches an effect on or off, and SEL grabs an effect so SELECT can move it in the chain. SEQ turns the sequencer on. REC then puts each note you play into the next step, and the white keys light up as step LEDs. Hold SAVE to keep your sound in this browser.
