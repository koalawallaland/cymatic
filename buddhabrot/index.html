How the fractal forms

Choose a complex parameter c and iterate z → z² + c, beginning at zero. For escaping orbits, accumulate each visited pixel into a density histogram. Millions of such orbits form this shape. Nebulabrot maps different escape depths to separate color channels.

This is a mathematical visualization. It does not measure external energy or establish contact with anything.
