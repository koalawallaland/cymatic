# Field guide to Koala Walla Land / Cymatic Studio

An interactive digital studio, part personal world, part experimental instrument. Start at the [entrance](https://koalawallaland.github.io/cymatic/), open the heart-shaped lock, and explore the desk.

## A suggested first visit

1. **Enter the studio.** The shoji-screen entrance is a threshold, not a conventional menu. The lock opens the desk.
2. **Explore the desk.** Objects are invitations: try the keyboard, audio objects, journal, and workstations. Some open separate experiences.
3. **Make a sound.** Visit the [Koe instrument](https://koalawallaland.github.io/cymatic/koe/) and experiment with the stylophone and its sound banks.
4. **Read something.** The journal is part of the world, not an external announcement board.
5. **Investigate.** The [experiments](https://koalawallaland.github.io/cymatic/experiments/) are places to play with perception, sound, and visual response.

## How to interpret the world

The desk is the interface: everyday objects act as portals into creative processes. Sound is both material and feedback. Repetition, memory, resonance, play, and tactile interaction connect the spaces.

This is not a claim that every interaction is a scientific instrument. Artistic simulations, experiments, and working tools should be understood on their own terms; individual experiences may be unfinished.

## Accessibility and orientation

- Try keyboard focus (Tab and Enter) where controls support it.
- Motion-sensitive visitors can enable their device's **Reduce Motion** preference; the entrance has a reduced-motion path.
- Some audio requires a tap to start because browsers restrict autoplay.
- If an object does not respond, try its dedicated page or report the issue through GitHub.
