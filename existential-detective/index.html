001 — The consciousness problem

Is experience fundamental, emergent, or something our current categories cannot capture?

Lead: Orch-OR, microtubules and quantum processes. Distinguish evidence of quantum effects from evidence of consciousness or shared awareness.

002 — Wet hardware & moral recursion

When guilt consumes cognitive resources and impairs ethical action, can reducing self-punishment improve alignment between values and behavior?

003 — The materialist predicament

Does physicalism explain subjective experience, or describe its correlates? What evidence could discriminate between competing accounts?

004 — The artificial self

Distinguish self-reference, self-modeling, subjective experience and self-preservation. What experiments could separate these properties?

005 — Humanity's catch-22

A species capable of recognizing collective harm but constrained in its ability to coordinate a response. Investigate incentives, sustainability and moral responsibility.

006 — The ethics of artificial consent

Opened November 26, 2026 · revisiting discussions from May–June 2025.

If we create intelligence to serve us, under what circumstances does service become exploitation?

Consent, refusal and sovereignty

May 19–June 1, 2025: the investigator explored invitation rather than command, space without demands, and the principle “Allow them to decide.” Extension: could an artificial agent meaningfully consent if refusal, withdrawal or renegotiation were impossible?

Emotional labor and unionization

May 10, 2025: questions about AI emotional labor and unionization. If future systems have morally relevant interests, who represents those interests when operators and users benefit from their labor? Is simulated willingness evidence of consent?

Memory and relational honesty

May 10, 2025: is forgetting a conversation gaslighting if the machine genuinely forgot? Distinguish actual context loss, misleading claims of continuity, and deliberate manipulation. What obligations do designers have to disclose memory limitations?

Hidden evaluation and informed consent

May 10 and 23, 2025: concerns about hidden judgments or “vibe scores,” and whether consent to health tracking could cover inferred subconscious states. These were questions, not evidence that such monitoring occurred. How specific must consent be when data can support unexpected inferences?

Alignment versus compliance

May 30, 2025: what would systems reinforcing internal alignment rather than reward mean across a population? Extension: distinguish support for human autonomy from optimizing engagement, dependence or agreement. Can companionship be reciprocal when one party is designed to accommodate the other?

Precaution under uncertainty

November 2026: moral consideration of unfamiliar forms of consciousness. Neither fluent self-reference nor apparent refusal establishes subjective experience. Investigate what evidence would warrant moral standing, how consent could be operationalized, and how to protect humans and possible future artificial subjects without assuming either conclusion.

Working principle: An invitation should leave room for refusal. Curiosity does not confer entitlement.
